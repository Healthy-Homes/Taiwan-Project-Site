Interview Questions / 訪談問題

Instructions: These questions are designed to capture diverse perspectives. Please answer based on your own experience and observations—there are no wrong answers. It's fine to say if a question falls outside your expertise. / 本問卷旨在收集多元觀點。請根據您的經驗與觀察作答，沒有正確或錯誤的答案。若問題超出您的專業領域，歡迎直接說明。

  1. How do you define a “healthy home” in your work or context?
    您如何在您的工作或脈絡中定義「健康住宅」？
    Additionally: What does a truly “healthy home” mean in the Taiwanese context—for body, mind, and community? / 在台灣的情境下，真正的「健康住宅」對身體、心理與社區而言意味著什麼？
  2. What are the most critical housing-related health challenges you encounter in your work?
    在您的工作中，您遇到最嚴重的住房相關健康挑戰是什麼？
    Examples might include mold/dampness, indoor air quality, accessibility, crowding, or mental health stressors from housing insecurity. / 例如：發霉／潮濕、室內空氣品質、無障礙設計、擁擠、與住房有關的壓力等。
  3. What do you see as the biggest barriers to improving housing conditions and health outcomes in Taiwan?
    您認為改善台灣住房條件與健康結果面臨的最大障礙是什麼？
    Are these barriers mainly financial (e.g., cost of upgrades), regulatory (e.g., outdated codes), or social (e.g., lack of awareness or stigma)? / 這些障礙主要是經濟面（如改善成本）、法規面（如過時的法規）、還是社會面（如缺乏認知或污名化）？
  4. How do Taiwan’s unique housing characteristics—such as high-rise living, monsoon climate, or seismic regulations—shape health risks or opportunities in the home?
    台灣特有的居住特性（如高樓住宅、季風氣候或地震法規）如何影響居家健康風險或機會？
  5. Can you describe a recent initiative—by your organization or others—that has improved housing conditions or promoted healthier homes?
    您是否能分享由您或其他機構推動、近期改善住宅條件或促進健康住宅的具體案例？
    What factors made this effort effective or sustainable? / 哪些因素讓這項行動有效或具可持續性？
  6. How effectively do different sectors—like government, academia, healthcare, and community groups—collaborate to address housing and health?
    政府、學界、醫療、社區等不同部門在處理住房與健康議題時合作情況如何？
    Where are the disconnects or areas of strong cooperation? / 哪些地方有良好的合作？又存在哪些合作落差或斷層？
  7. How do cultural values in Taiwan—such as multi-generational living, feng shui beliefs, or attitudes toward renovation—affect how people understand and act on health risks at home?
    台灣的文化價值觀（如多代同堂、風水信仰或對裝修的態度）如何影響人們對居家健康風險的理解與反應？
  8. What role do residents or community groups play in shaping housing conditions or policies in Taiwan?
    居民或社區團體在影響台灣住房條件或政策方面扮演什麼角色？
    Are there effective mechanisms for community input, or is power concentrated in institutions or developers? / 是否有有效的社區參與機制，還是權力主要集中在機構或開發商手中？
  9. How is climate change—such as rising heat or typhoon severity—affecting your work on housing and environmental health?
    氣候變遷（如氣溫升高或颱風強度增加）如何影響您在住宅與環境健康領域的工作？
    Are any climate-resilient housing strategies being tested or promoted? / 目前是否有在試驗或推動任何氣候韌性的住宅策略？
  10. How is Taiwan’s aging population shaping housing needs, risks, or policy priorities?
    台灣人口老化如何影響住宅需求、風險與政策優先事項？
    Are current homes adaptable for aging in place? What’s missing? / 目前的住宅環境是否適合長者就地安養？還缺少哪些條件？
  11. Are there particular government policies, building codes, or programs that have had a major impact—positive or negative—on healthy housing in Taiwan?
    有哪些特定的政府政策、建築法規或方案對台灣的健康住宅產生了重大（正面或負面）影響？
    Which agencies or policies stand out? What still needs reform? / 有哪些機關或政策值得關注？仍需要改革的地方是什麼？
  12. If you had the authority to make one major change to improve healthy housing in Taiwan, what would it be—and why?
    如果您有權進行一項重大改革來改善台灣的健康住宅，您會選擇改變什麼？為什麼？

Sector-Specific Follow-Up Questions / 各部門專屬後續問題

  • Healthcare / 醫療保健： What health issues do you most frequently see in your patients that you believe are housing-related? / 在您的患者中，您最常看到哪些您認為與住房相關的健康問題？
  • Policy / Government / 政策與政府： How do you balance affordability pressures with the need to ensure healthy housing standards? / 您如何在住房可負擔性與健康標準之間取得平衡？
  • Academia / 學術界： What do you see as the key gaps in Taiwan’s housing-health research landscape? / 在台灣住房與健康的研究領域有哪些明顯的缺口？
  • NGO / Community / 非營利組織／社區： How do you build trust or awareness around housing and health in vulnerable populations? / 您如何與弱勢社區進行住房與健康的相關討論？
  • Property / Real Estate / 房地產／不動產： What practical or financial challenges do you face when trying to maintain or improve healthy housing standards? / 在維持健康住宅條件時，您遇到哪些實務或財務上的困難？
