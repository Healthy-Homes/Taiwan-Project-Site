Interview Questions 問卷問題

1. Healthy Home 定義

How do you define a “healthy home” in your work or context?
您如何在您的工作或脈絡中定義「健康住宅」？

Additionally: Beyond just the absence of hazards, what does a truly “healthy home” mean in the Taiwanese context—for body, mind, and community?
除了沒有危害之外，在台灣的情境下，真正的「健康住宅」對身體、心理與社區而言意味著什麼？

2. Housing Health Challenges 住房相關健康挑戰

What are the most critical housing-related health challenges you encounter in your work?
在您的工作中，您遇到最嚴重的住房相關健康挑戰是什麼？

Examples: mold, indoor air quality, accessibility, crowding, housing-related stress.
例如：發霉／潮濕、室內空氣品質、無障礙設計、擁擠、與住房有關的壓力等。

3. Barriers to Improvement 改善障礙

What do you see as the biggest barriers to improving housing conditions and health outcomes in Taiwan?
您認為改善台灣住房條件與健康結果面臨的最大障礙是什麼？

Are they mainly financial, regulatory, or social?
這些障礙主要是經濟面、法規面，還是社會面？

4. Taiwan-specific Housing Risks 台灣特有住宅風險

How do Taiwan’s unique housing characteristics—such as high-rise living, monsoon climate, or seismic regulations—shape health risks or opportunities in the home?
台灣特有的居住特性如何影響居家健康風險或機會？

5. Example of Success 成功案例

Can you describe a recent initiative—by your organization or others—that has improved housing conditions or promoted healthier homes?
您是否能分享近期改善住宅條件或促進健康住宅的具體案例？

What made this effort effective or sustainable?
哪些因素讓這項行動有效或具可持續性？

6. Cross-sector Collaboration 跨部門合作

How effectively do different sectors—like government, academia, healthcare, and community groups—collaborate to address housing and health?
不同部門在處理住房與健康議題時合作情況如何？

Where are the disconnects or strengths?
哪些地方有良好的合作？又存在哪些合作落差或斷層？

7. Cultural Influences 文化影響

How do cultural values in Taiwan—such as multi-generational living, feng shui beliefs, or attitudes toward renovation—affect how people understand and act on health risks at home?
台灣的文化價值觀如何影響人們對居家健康風險的理解與反應？

8. Community Power 社區參與

What role do residents or community groups play in shaping housing conditions or policies in Taiwan?
居民或社區團體在影響台灣住房條件或政策方面扮演什麼角色？

Are there effective mechanisms for input?
是否有有效的社區參與機制？

9. Climate Change and Housing 氣候變遷與住宅

How is climate change—such as rising heat or typhoon severity—affecting your work on housing and environmental health?
氣候變遷如何影響您在住宅與環境健康領域的工作？

10. Aging Population and Housing 人口老化與住宅

How is Taiwan’s aging population shaping housing needs, risks, or policy priorities?
台灣人口老化如何影響住宅需求、風險與政策優先事項？

11. Policy and Code Impacts 政策與法規影響

Are there particular government policies, building codes, or programs that have had a major impact—positive or negative—on healthy housing in Taiwan?
有哪些特定的政策或法規對健康住宅產生重大影響？

12. One Change to Improve Housing 改革建議

If you had the authority to make one major change to improve healthy housing in Taiwan, what would it be—and why?
如果您有權改善健康住宅，您會選擇改變什麼？為什麼？
