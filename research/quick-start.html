Quick Start: Researcher Toolkit

Use this toolkit to support interviews, note-taking, and consent during the Healthy Homes Taiwan project.

While the study is designed to understand housing and health broadly, particular attention is given to issues affecting vulnerable groups such as low-income households, older adults, and residents facing housing insecurity.

Refer to the tools below for links, recording tips, and templates.

🧭 Field Prep Checklist

  • Review Consent Form (EN) and Consent Form (中文)
  • Open Interview Questions on mobile or tablet
  • Launch Vocaroo, Otter, or your preferred recorder
  • Direct survey responses to Submit Page

🎙 Recording Tips

  • Ask for consent at the beginning of every recording
  • Note file name + participant code aloud
  • Place phone/mic on a soft surface to reduce vibration
  • Use the question list as a rough guide, not a script
