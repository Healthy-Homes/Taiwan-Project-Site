Project Title: Healthy Homes Taiwan – Fulbright Research Study

Researcher: Matthew Ulsh, U.S. Fulbright Public Health Fellow

Contact: matthew.ulsh@fulbrightmail.org

Purpose of the Study

This study explores how housing conditions impact health in Taiwan, and what efforts are underway to improve them. It includes interviews with professionals, residents, and experts in sectors such as healthcare, government, academia, and community work.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is completely voluntary. You may decline to answer any question and can stop the interview or survey at any time without penalty.

Confidentiality and Identification

You can choose how you would like to be identified in the research outputs:

  • By name and title (attributed)
  • By a general role only (e.g., “Taipei housing official”)
  • Completely anonymous (quotes de-identified)

Recording and Use of Responses

With your permission, your interview may be audio-recorded for accuracy. All recordings and data will be stored securely. Selected responses may be quoted in research reports, but will respect your chosen level of privacy.

Contact and Questions

You can contact the researcher with any questions, concerns, or to withdraw participation at any time.
