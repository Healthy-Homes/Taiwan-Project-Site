Healthy Homes Taiwan: Research Portal

This research is part of a Fulbright-supported public health project conducted in collaboration with National Taiwan University.

This research focuses especially on how housing conditions affect the health of low-income and disadvantaged communities in Taiwan. However, all perspectives are welcome, and responses from across sectors and roles are highly valued.

Participants from all backgrounds are encouraged to share their experiences and insights.
