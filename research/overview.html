📚 Research Overview / 研究簡介

This Fulbright-supported public health research examines how housing and environmental factors affect health—particularly for low-income and disadvantaged populations in Taiwan—and how lessons might inform healthier housing practices globally.

本研究由富布賴特資助，聚焦於住宅與環境因素如何影響健康，特別是對低收入與弱勢群體的影響，並希望藉此提供台灣與全球健康住宅實踐的相互借鏡。

🔍 Guiding Questions / 研究核心問題

  1. How is “healthy housing” defined, operationalized, and supported across sectors in Taipei/Taiwan, and what lessons can be drawn for implementation in U.S. cities?
    在台北／台灣，「健康住宅」是如何被定義、具體實踐，並在不同部門中被推動的？從中可獲得哪些可供美國城市借鑑的經驗？

  2. How does cultural context influence perceptions of health risks in the home environment, and how can these insights inform risk communication and intervention design?
    台灣的文化脈絡如何影響人們對居家健康風險的理解？這些洞察可如何應用於風險溝通與介入措施的設計？
